# Akash Jha

India is assembling the operating system of a civilization. UPI for money. GST and MCA for business truth. DigiLocker for documents. A billion phones as the terminal, more of them NPU-equipped every quarter. The rails are live at national scale.

The software of the next decade runs on these rails — and almost none of it has been written yet.

I intend to write load-bearing parts of it.

## The thesis

India built the base rails — identity, payments, documents, tax. What's missing is the layer above them: the software that turns national infrastructure into working products for a billion people. That layer is where I build. Wherever a rail exists and the software native to it doesn't — that's the territory.

Two rules govern everything on it. Sovereignty is an architecture decision, not a slogan — built in India, governed by Indian law, data that stays with its owner. And Indian scale is the hardest testbed on earth: software that survives here ships anywhere. Built for India first. Built to travel.

## Current fronts

**Compliance rails.** Every registered business in India leaves a machine-readable trail — filings, invoices, registries. Structured properly, that trail becomes the trust layer of Indian commerce: verification, credit, discovery. Payments got its rails. Compliance is next. I'm building it.

**Education.** How a few hundred million Indians learn is being decided right now — vernacular-first, mobile-native, built from the ground up. The defaults set this decade will hold for a generation. I'm building for that decade, not this quarter.

**Work.** Indian hiring still runs on proxies — degrees, brand names, who you know. The rails to prove skill directly now exist: verifiable credentials, on-record work, portable reputation. The bridge from learning to earning is unbuilt infrastructure. I'm building the bridge.

**On-device AI.** Indian AI will not run in someone else's cloud. The fleet is already in pockets — hundreds of millions of NPU-equipped phones. The endgame is an AI agent working for the kirana owner in Mangalpur — inventory, credit, GST, supplier follow-ups — running on the phone in his pocket, in his language, his data never leaving his hands. The fleet exists. The agent doesn't. Yet.

These are the fronts open today. The thesis is bigger than any of them.

## Why me

I've shipped on these rails before. Built a GST filing engine that automated returns at one-tenth the market price — and exited it. Built a compliance platform that five lakh Indian small businesses ran on. Years spent reverse-engineering how India's business record actually gets made — filings, registries, invoice trails — at the depth where you can build systems on it, not write think-pieces about it. Most developers treat the regulatory layer as someone else's problem. I read it as an API surface.
